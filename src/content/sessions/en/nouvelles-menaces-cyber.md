**Skills, speed, autonomy: a change of scale**

Artificial intelligence could upend the cyberattack landscape. By making certain skills more accessible, speeding up the exploitation of vulnerabilities and paving the way for more autonomous attacks, it changes the game. But does the real threat lie in new kinds of attacks… or in their ability to multiply at an unprecedented scale?

**About the speaker Régis Dubrulle**

Régis Dubrulle is the ANSSI delegate for the Pays de la Loire region. With 25 years of experience in information systems and cybersecurity, he was notably the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) of the agri-food group Terrena. Today, as regional delegate, he works in synergy with existing regional bodies and authorities to prevent incidents and raise awareness of good IT practices among local public and private players.
