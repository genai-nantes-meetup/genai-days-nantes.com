Clever Cloud’s podcast “Message à caractère informatique” records live during the Afterwork. The idea: technical people (developers, devops, sysadmins, CTOs…) talk about the tech news of the moment, informally.

The format is very open. Each participant brings a few news links they want to discuss; after a round of introductions, the conversation follows those topics wherever they lead. It stays a technical conversation between peers: everyone shares their expertise, with no marketing pitch or sales talk. The recording is in French.

Around the mic, with full profiles below under “On stage”:

- **Horacio Gonzalez**, Clever Cloud, host
- **Florian Bruniaux**, RTK
- **Olivier Lauvray**, VSORA
- **Théo Hubert**, AlphaEdge
- **David Pilato**, Elastic
- **Roch Molléro**, Gobano Robotics

Attendees can watch it being recorded and talk with the guests while they network.
