Thomas Mathieu (Guest Suite, French Tech Nantes) and Mathieu Le Gac (Wattflow) are back from a week in China with the Pays de la Loire Region delegation: Shenzhen, Shanghai and Hangzhou, with visits to Alibaba, Tencent, Agibot, JD.com and Hangcha.

They came back with one observation. Over there, millions of people already use AI agents without even knowing it: the Qwen app orders a coffee, does the shopping or books a flight from a single sentence. Humanoid robots are entering factories, and models are released as open weight because the business is elsewhere. Here, we still wonder which use case to hand over to AI. There, the question is rather: which task can the agent not do yet?

They share what they saw, what changed their minds, and what they keep at arm’s length: an official delegation sees what its hosts choose to show. And what a European executive can take from it right now: choose your dependencies, keep a way out, and stop looking for the use case.
