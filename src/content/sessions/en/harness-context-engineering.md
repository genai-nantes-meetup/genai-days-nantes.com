How to design the harness around an AI agent: tool selection, memory and orchestration. The amount of context you inject is just one lever, and more of it can make results worse.

The aim goes beyond staying effective over time: it is to put together all the tasks that let the agent reach its objective cheaply, quickly and with a low error rate.
