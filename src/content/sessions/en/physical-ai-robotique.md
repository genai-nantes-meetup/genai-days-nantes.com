This talk traces the historical advances that gave rise to Physical AI and that now let robots learn to act in the real world: learning from demonstration, vision-language-action models (VLA), world models and reinforcement learning (RL). It then shows how Gobano Robotics assembles these building blocks to automate industrial manipulation tasks, especially with soft materials. Through demos and deployment examples, the talk explores the path from a learned gesture to autonomous work, with its challenges of reliability, error recovery and useful throughput, and many videos of the process.
