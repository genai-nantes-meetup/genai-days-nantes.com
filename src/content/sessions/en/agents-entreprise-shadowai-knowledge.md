How to regain control over unmanaged ChatGPT use in the workplace, weighing the risk of data leaks against the opportunity to build internal AI agents that are governed and connected to the company’s knowledge base.

Between an outright ban and a hands-off approach, this session explores a third way: giving teams governed AI tools instead of leaving them to tinker on their own.
