What if marketing teams spent 80% of their day in a terminal? A few years ago, nobody would have believed it. The developers’ black screen, the one most people close in a panic, has become their workspace. And marketing is only the start: sales, ops, product. The terminal is no longer just for engineers.

You know the routine: explain your context again in every new conversation, paste the same information into ChatGPT, then Claude, then Notion, and start over the next day. Clément, marketing lead at lemlist, and Louis, growth at Clever Cloud, stopped doing that. They built a “second brain”: an external memory where they store, once and for all, what they know about their work, and that their AI agents read before every task.

How to set it up, how to structure it, which tools and method to use, and how to make sure the agent actually uses it: they show it all. Above all, they share what it changed in the way they work.
