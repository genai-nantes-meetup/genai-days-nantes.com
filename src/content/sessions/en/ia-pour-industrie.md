An introduction to industrial robotics: what a robot can do today, and what still holds it back.

AI expands what robots can do. Perception, adaptation, decision-making: it gives robots the capabilities they lacked to move beyond repetitive, strictly programmed tasks.

That leaves the question that matters to an executive: what would a humanoid robot actually do in a factory, and for what gains? This session reviews the use cases already in place on production lines.
